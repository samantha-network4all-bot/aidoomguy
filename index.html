AI Doomguy

AI Doomguy is a hobby project: an AI agent that teaches itself to play the classic game Doom (1993). It sees the screen, builds its own map, plans its own routes and remembers its mistakes. Nobody holds the controller.

Every day the project picks the most interesting real moment of that day's gameplay (finding a keycard, surviving with low health, a secret area) and turns it into a short vertical clip. The gameplay is always shown at real speed.

The AI Doomguy poster app

A small script on the project owner's own computer publishes that daily clip to the project's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It posts at most one video per day, only to that one account, and only content the project made itself. It does not read, collect or analyse anyone else's data.

Videos also appear on YouTube: AI Doomguy.
