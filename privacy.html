Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The AI Doomguy poster app ("the app") is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by the owner of the AI Doomguy project to publish the project's own gameplay clips to the project's own TikTok account.

What data the app uses

  • The access token, refresh token and TikTok account identifier (open_id) that TikTok returns when the owner authorises the app. These are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner, and are used only to publish videos to that same account.
  • The account's posting options (privacy levels, maximum video length) as returned by TikTok, used only to choose valid settings for each post.
  • The status of each upload, used only to confirm that a post succeeded.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, read, store, sell or share data about any other TikTok user. It does not read other users' videos, comments, followers or messages. It does not use any data for advertising, profiling or training of AI models. There are no other users of the app.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the app is in use and can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions). Revoking access, or deleting the local token file, removes all data the app holds.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's YouTube channel AI Doomguy.
